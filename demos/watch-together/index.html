Watch Together — Engineering Architecture

High-Concurrency Synchronized Streaming & Voice Engine

1. Authoritative Clock & Micro-Drift Correction

The room host acts as the authoritative master clock. Client drift is continually computed via periodic NTP-style ping calculations. When delta is under 1.5 seconds, playback rate is micro-adjusted (0.95x - 1.05x) so audio never hiccups or stutters; larger deltas trigger seamless seek-snapping.

2. WebRTC Full-Mesh Voice Topology

Voice channels use peer-to-peer WebRTC mesh routing for groups up to 12 participants. Signaling and ICE exchange are orchestrated over Socket.IO, resulting in sub-30ms round-trip audio latency with built-in voice activity detection (VAD).

3. Persistent SQLite Engine & Directional Action Cooldowns

Built on Node.js native DatabaseSync (SQLite). Action cooldowns and sequence counters prevent cascade ping-pong echo loops during simultaneous play/pause toggles by different participants.
